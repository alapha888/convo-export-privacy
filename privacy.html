Privacy Policy — ConvoExport

Effective date: November 15, 2026 · Applies to ConvoExport version 1.0.0 and later 1.x versions.

The short version

ConvoExport has no account, no analytics, and no server. It makes no network requests at all. Your conversations are read only inside your own browser, turned into an export file on your device, and never collected, transmitted, sold, or shared. Pro activation is verified offline on your device (see below); no license key is ever sent anywhere.

What is stored, and where

ConvoExport stores only its own settings in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local): your export format and filename preferences, your remaining free-export count for the day, and — if you activate Pro — your Pro license key and its locally verified status. Exported files are saved to your device through the browser's download function, only when you click Export. No conversation content is stored by the extension itself.

What is NOT collected

  • No personal information, no identifiers, no usage statistics, no crash reports.
  • No browsing history. The extension only runs on ChatGPT and Claude pages, and only to read the conversation you ask it to export.
  • No cookies, no third-party scripts, no remote calls of any kind — including for Pro activation (see below).

Conversation content

When you click Export, the extension's content script reads the conversation visible on the ChatGPT or Claude page you are viewing, converts it to Markdown, plain text, Word, or PDF (via the print view), and downloads the file to your device. This processing happens entirely on your device; conversation content is never copied out, stored separately, or sent anywhere. The extension does not read any page other than the ChatGPT or Claude conversation tab you export from.

Pro license verification (offline — no network call)

Pro is optional. If you buy Pro, you receive an offline activation key. When you paste the key into the extension, it is verified locally on your device using a built-in public key; the key is never transmitted to any server, and the extension makes no network request to check it. The key and its verified status are stored only in your browser's local storage. The seller keeps its own purchase records under the seller's own privacy policy; ConvoExport itself holds no copy.

Permissions and why each is needed

Host access: chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.aiRun the content script on ChatGPT and Claude conversation pages only, to read the conversation you ask to export (see "Conversation content" above).

Deleting your data

Clear the extension's settings from its options page, or remove the extension from your browser — uninstalling deletes all locally stored data with it. We run no server, so there is no server copy of your data to request deletion of. Exported files on your device are yours to delete like any other file.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes any of the above (for example, an optional online feature), this page will be updated before that version ships, and any feature that transmits data will be opt-in and off by default.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support/feedback channel on the extension's store listing page.

隐私政策 — ConvoExport

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 15 日 · 适用于 ConvoExport 1.0.0 及后续 1.x 版本。

一句话版本

ConvoExport 没有账号、没有任何统计、没有自己的服务器， 不发出任何网络请求。你的对话只在你自己的浏览器内被读取、转换成导出文件 保存在你的设备上，绝不会被收集、传输、出售或分享。Pro 激活在你的设备上离线验证 （见下文）；激活钥匙从不发送到任何地方。

保存什么、存在哪里

ConvoExport 只在浏览器本地存储（chrome.storage.local）中保存它自己的 设置：导出格式与文件名偏好、当日剩余免费导出次数，以及（如已激活 Pro）Pro 激活钥匙 与本地验证状态。导出文件仅在你点击「导出」时通过浏览器下载功能保存到你的设备。 扩展本身不保存任何对话内容。

不收集什么

  • 不收集个人信息、标识符、使用统计、崩溃报告，一概不收集。
  • 不记录浏览历史。扩展只在 ChatGPT 与 Claude 页面上运行，且只读取你要导出的那段对话。
  • 无 Cookie、无第三方脚本、无任何远程调用——包括 Pro 激活（见下文）。

对话内容如何处理

当你点击「导出」，扩展的内容脚本读取你正在查看的 ChatGPT 或 Claude 页面上的对话， 转换成 Markdown、纯文本、Word 或 PDF（经打印视图），并下载到你的设备。处理完全在本地 进行；对话内容不会被复制出去、另行存储或发送到任何地方。扩展不会读取除你要导出的 ChatGPT / Claude 对话标签页之外的任何页面。

Pro 钥匙校验（离线，不联网）

Pro 为可选付费功能。购买后你会收到一个离线激活钥匙；粘贴到扩展后，钥匙使用内置公钥 在你的设备上本地验证，从不传输到任何服务器，扩展也不会发出任何网络请求 去校验它。钥匙与验证状态只保存在你的浏览器本地存储中。卖家按其自身隐私政策保留购买 记录；ConvoExport 本身不持有任何副本。

删除数据

可在扩展设置页清除设置；或直接卸载扩展——卸载即删除全部本地数据。我们没有服务器， 因此你的数据不存在需要申请删除的服务器副本。已导出到你设备上的文件由你自行删除。

政策变更

若未来版本改变以上任何一条（例如增加可选在线功能），本页会在该版本发布前更新， 且任何会传输数据的功能都将默认关闭、需你主动开启。
